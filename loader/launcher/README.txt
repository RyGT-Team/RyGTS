background.png is the picture behind the RyGTS launcher's home view. To use your own, replace it with
any PNG or JPG of the same name (16:9 looks best); several (background.png, background2.png, ...) fade
from one to the next. Without one, the launcher uses GT Sport's own picture (pic1.png) from your game
files, or a plain dark background.

logo.png, when present, is shown in place of the "GRAN TURISMO SPORT" title (on the home view and in
the settings). Without it the title is drawn in text.
